# SpendWise – Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** November 13, 2026

**Developer:** Nikshay

**Application:** SpendWise (Android)

**Tagline:** Your money. Your control.

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to SpendWise.

SpendWise is a personal finance management application designed to help users manage their income, expenses, financial accounts, monthly budgets, and transaction history.

Your financial privacy is important. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information, what privacy-related features are available, and how users can manage their financial data.

By using SpendWise, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information and Financial Data

SpendWise may store financial information that users manually enter into the application, including:

- Account names and account details.
- Account balances.
- Income and expense transactions.
- Transaction descriptions and notes.
- Transaction categories.
- Monthly financial summaries.
- Account transfers.
- Application preferences and settings.

This information is used to provide the application's financial management functionality.

Users should avoid entering passwords, banking credentials, card PINs, or other highly sensitive authentication information into transaction descriptions or notes.

## 3. Data Storage

SpendWise is designed around an offline-first approach.

Financial information is intended to be stored locally within the application's private storage on the Android device.

The application is designed to provide its core financial management functionality without requiring users to create an online account.

Users are responsible for maintaining their own backups of important financial information.

## 4. Internet Connectivity and Data Sharing

SpendWise is intended primarily for local financial management.

The application does not require cloud synchronization to provide its core functionality.

Any network communication, third-party integrations, analytics, crash reporting, or external services, if implemented, must be assessed separately and disclosed accurately.

The developer does not claim that information is never transmitted without verifying the application's actual network behaviour and dependencies.

## 5. Application Permissions

SpendWise may use Android permissions or system capabilities to support specific application features.

The following describes the intended purposes of relevant capabilities:

### Notifications

If notification functionality is enabled, Android notification permission may be requested to display application notifications.

Users can manage notification permissions through Android system settings.

### Biometric Authentication

SpendWise may use Android's biometric authentication facilities to provide an additional application access mechanism.

Biometric authentication is handled through Android's supported authentication mechanisms. The application does not need access to the user's fingerprint or facial biometric templates.

### Device Security

The application may use Android security mechanisms to protect access to financial information and restrict screenshots where supported.

### Storage and Backup

Backup and restore functionality may require access to user-selected files through Android's document picker or other supported system interfaces.

The application should only access files required for the user's requested operation.

*Actual permissions depend on the installed application version and its AndroidManifest.xml.*

## 6. Application Lock and Security

SpendWise provides privacy-related controls intended to protect access to financial information.

These may include:

- PIN-based application locking.
- Biometric authentication.
- Configurable application lock behaviour.
- Immediate application lock timeout.
- Screenshot protection.
- Configurable notification privacy.

Users should keep their device operating system updated and protect their device with a secure screen lock.

No software application can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Backup and Restore

SpendWise includes backup and restore functionality intended to help users preserve their financial information.

Users may create backups and restore previously saved application data.

Password protection may be available for backup files.

Users are responsible for:

- Keeping backup files in secure locations.
- Protecting backup passwords.
- Avoiding unauthorized sharing of backup files.
- Maintaining additional copies of important financial information.

Deleting the application or clearing its data may remove locally stored financial information. External backup files may remain wherever the user saved them.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Financial information stored locally remains available until it is deleted through the application, removed through Android application-data controls, or otherwise removed from the device.

Uninstalling SpendWise may remove its locally stored application data.

Backup files exported outside the application are not automatically deleted when the application is uninstalled.

Users should manage exported backups separately.

## 9. Children's Privacy

SpendWise is a general personal finance management application and is not specifically designed for children.

The application is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children through an online account service.

Any third-party libraries, services, or integrations included in SpendWise may operate under their respective privacy policies and terms.

Users should review applicable third-party policies where relevant.

The developer should disclose any third-party data collection or transmission introduced in future application versions.
